# 📱 Swaktorie Meals: Social Media Command Center Guide

*A complete manual for managing the Swaktorie Facebook Page, generating on-brand content, and automating Messenger replies.*

## 🚀 Getting Started

The **Social Media Command Center** is a powerful, offline tool built perfectly for the Swaktorie team. It runs entirely in your web browser — no login, no internet connection required, and it never crashes.

**To open it:** Double-click the `swaktorie_social_dashboard.html` file in your project folder.

---

## 🛠️ The 7 Core Tools

### 1. 📝 Post Generator
*Stop staring at a blank screen. Generate ready-to-post Facebook captions with emojis and hashtags in seconds.*

1.  **Select a Post Type:** Choose from 7 types (e.g., *Meal Spotlight*, *Customer Testimonial*, *Health Tip*).
2.  **Add Details (Optional):** Type in the specific meal name or a custom detail you want to mention.
3.  **Choose a Tone:** Pick your vibe:
    *   😊 **Friendly:** Warm and approachable (Best for everyday posts).
    *   💼 **Professional:** Highlights the clinical and chef-curated aspects.
    *   🔥 **Hype:** High energy! (Best for new drops or big announcements).
    *   📖 **Educational:** Focuses on nutrition science.
4.  **Action:** Click **✨ Generate Post** and then **📋 Copy Caption** to paste directly into Facebook!

### 2. 🎯 Promo Engine
*Create compelling special offers and instantly get both the Facebook Post and the matching Messenger auto-reply.*

1.  **Select Promo Type:** Choose between *Payday Sale*, *First-Time Buyer*, *Holiday Special*, etc.
2.  **Set the Rules:** Enter the Discount % (e.g., 15), Promo Code (e.g., SWELDO15), and Expiry Date.
3.  **Action:** Click **🎯 Generate Promo**. You will receive:
    *   A hyped Facebook Caption to announce the sale.
    *   A Messenger Reply script that explains the promo terms when people message you.

### 3. 📅 Content Calendar
*Organize your marketing week visually and export everything for Meta Business Suite.*

1.  **Plan:** Add specific posts to the Monday-Sunday calendar grids.
2.  **Auto-Plan:** Don't know what to post? Click **✨ Auto-Fill Suggested** for a proven, balanced week of content (Spotlights, Polls, Tips).
3.  **Action:** Click **📥 Export CSV** to download a spreadsheet you can upload directly to Facebook for bulk scheduling.

### 4. 💬 Comment & Messenger Templates
*Never type the same reply twice. Standardize your customer service.*

1.  **Comment Replies:** Pre-written responses for common scenarios like New Inquiries, Pricing Questions, Delivery Areas, and Complaint Handling.
2.  **Messenger Flow Builder:** A sequence of 4 steps (Welcome, Menu, Delivery, Away Message) that you can copy exactly into Facebook's "Automated Responses" settings.

### 5. 📊 Analytics Tracker
*Keep an eye on what's working without getting lost in Facebook's overwhelming data dashboard.*

1.  **Log Data:** At the end of every week, enter your Page Reach, Engagement, and Orders into the form.
2.  **Track:** It will immediately update the real-time bar chart showing your growth trend right inside the dashboard.
3.  *Note: Your data is saved locally in your browser.*

### 6. 📋 Caption Library
*A searchable database of 50+ evergreen post captions.*

1.  **Search:** Look for keywords like "protein" or "weekend".
2.  **Filter:** Filter by category (Morning, Evening, Health Tip, Customer Love).
3.  **Favorite:** Click the ⭐️ icon to save your most-used captions so they are always at the top of the list next time.

### 7. 🧮 BMI → Meal Plan Recommender (The Game Changer)
*Turn Messenger conversations into instant sales by giving clinical recommendations.*

1.  **Gather Info:** Ask the customer in Messenger for their Height, Weight, Age, and Gender.
2.  **Input:** Enter these details into the dashboard tool.
3.  **Calculate:** It instantly calculates their BMI, identifies their category (e.g., Overweight), calculates their daily calorie burn (TDEE), and **matches them to the exact Swaktorie plan** (e.g., Weight Loss Plan - ₱280/day).
4.  **Action:** Click **📋 Copy Messenger Reply** to paste a highly personalized, formatted response back to the customer recommending the perfect setup.

## 💡 Pro Tips for Meta Business Suite Integration

*   **Bulk Scheduling:** Export your calendar as a CSV and upload it to Meta Business Suite to schedule a whole week of posts in 5 minutes.
*   **Automated Responses:** Go to Meta Business Suite -> Automations. Paste the scripts from the **Comment & Messenger Templates** tab into your "Greet People" and "Frequently Asked Questions" sections.
*   **The Recommender:** Pin the `swaktorie_social_dashboard.html` bookmark to your browser so your staff always has the BMI Recommender tool just one click away when answering customer chats.
