Frequently Asked Questions

🍱 What exactly is "Meal Prep"? ▼
Meal prep is our clinical approach to nutrition. We cook, weigh, and package your meals for the following day or week, ensuring you hit your exact macro targets without the stress of cooking or calorie counting. Each container is labeled with precise calorie and macro information.
💳 How do I pay for my subscription? ▼
We support major Philippine digital wallets! Pay via GCash, Maya, or BDO/BPI Bank Transfer. We also accept Cash on Delivery (COD) for first-time weekly trials within San Rafael.
🚚 Where do you deliver? ▼
Our primary hubs are in San Rafael & Baliwag City, but we deliver to most areas in Bulacan and select areas in Metro Manila. Delivery fees depend on your exact location and will be calculated upon order confirmation. Delivery arrives every Sunday for the entire week.
📅 When is the order cutoff? ▼
Orders must be placed by every Thursday for delivery the following Sunday. This gives our chef time to source fresh ingredients and prepare your custom macros.
🥜 Can you accommodate allergies? ▼
Absolutely! During checkout, there's a special "Allergies & Notes" field where you can list any food restrictions. Our chef will adjust your meals accordingly. Common accommodations include nut-free, shellfish-free, and dairy-free modifications.
🔄 Can I cancel or pause my plan? ▼
Yes! You can pause or cancel your plan by messaging us on Facebook (@Swaktorie.Meals) or directly here on the website before the Thursday cutoff. We value flexibility — no lock-in contracts!
