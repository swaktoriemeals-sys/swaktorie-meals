Swaktorie Social HQ Manual

Your complete guide to managing the Facebook Page

🚀 Launch the Command Center

📝 1. Post Generator

Stop staring at a blank screen. Generate ready-to-post Facebook captions with emojis and hashtags in seconds.

  • Select a Post Type: Choose from 7 types (e.g., Meal Spotlight, Customer Testimonial).
  • Add Details: Type in the specific meal name or details.
  • Choose a Tone: Pick Friendly, Professional, Hype, or Educational.
  • Action: Click "Generate" and copy the caption right into Facebook!

🎯 2. Promo Engine

Create compelling special offers instantly.

  • Set the Rules: Enter the Discount %, Promo Code, and Expiry Date.
  • Action: Click "Generate" to get both a hyped Facebook Caption AND a matching Messenger Reply script.

🧮 3. BMI → Plan Recommender (Game Changer)

Turn Messenger conversations into instant sales by giving clinical recommendations.

  • Gather Info: Ask the customer for their Height, Weight, Age, and Gender in Facebook Messenger.
  • Calculate: Enter the info. The tool instantly calculates their BMI & Calories needed.
  • Action: Click "Copy Messenger Reply". It automatically recommends the exact Swaktorie plan (e.g., Weight Loss Plan - ₱280/day) based on their body type. Paste this directly to the customer!

📅 4. Content Calendar

Organize your marketing week visually.

  • Auto-Plan: Click "Auto-Fill Suggested" for a proven, balanced week of content (Spotlights, Polls, Tips).
  • Action: Click "Export CSV" to download a file you can upload directly to Meta Business Suite for bulk scheduling.

💬 5. Comment & Messenger Templates

Never type the same reply twice.

  • Comment Replies: Pre-written responses for common scenarios like Pricing, Delivery Areas, and Complaint Handling.
  • Messenger Flow Builder: Copy the exact 4-step sequence into Facebook's "Automated Responses" settings.

📊 6. Analytics Tracker

Keep an eye on what's working visually.

  • Log Data: At the end of every week, enter your Page Reach, Engagement, and Orders.
  • Track: The dashboard will draw a real-time bar chart showing your growth trend. No spreadsheets needed.

📋 7. Caption Library

A searchable database of 50+ evergreen post captions.

  • Search & Filter: Look for keywords like protein or filter by category (e.g., Morning, Weekend).
  • Favorite: Click the star icon to save your most-used captions permanently.
